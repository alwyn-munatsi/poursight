# Boerewors & Chips

**Category:** Grill  
**Price:** $6.00

Traditional farm-style boerewors sausage, coiled and grilled, served with a portion of chips.

## Ingredients
- Boerewors Sausage (0.3 kg per serving)
- Potatoes (0.3 kg per serving)
- Cooking Oil (0.03 litre per serving)

## Preparation

Grilled whole in a coil, sliced at the table, served with hand-cut chips.
