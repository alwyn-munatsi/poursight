# Grilled Chicken Quarter

**Category:** Grill  
**Price:** $6.50

A flame-grilled chicken quarter, marinated overnight in a peri-peri style basting.

## Ingredients
- Chicken Quarters (0.4 kg per serving)
- Peri-Peri Sauce (0.03 litre per serving)

## Preparation

Basted twice while grilling over open coals; served bone-in.
