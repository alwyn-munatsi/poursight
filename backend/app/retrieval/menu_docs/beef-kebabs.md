# Beef Kebabs

**Category:** Grill  
**Price:** $6.50

Skewered cubes of marinated beef, grilled with onion and pepper between the cubes.

## Ingredients
- Beef Kebab Cubes (0.3 kg per serving)
- Cooking Oil (0.02 litre per serving)

## Preparation

Marinated overnight, threaded onto skewers with vegetables, grilled to order.
