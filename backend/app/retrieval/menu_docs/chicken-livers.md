# Chicken Livers

**Category:** Starter  
**Price:** $4.00

Pan-fried chicken livers in a peri-peri sauce - a popular starter with regulars.

## Ingredients
- Chicken Livers (raw) (0.3 kg per serving)
- Cooking Oil (0.02 litre per serving)

## Preparation

Pan-fried hot and fast to keep the livers tender, finished with peri-peri sauce.
