# Beef Samosas

**Category:** Starter  
**Price:** $3.50

Pastry parcels filled with spiced minced beef, deep fried and served as a starter.

- Samosa Pastry & Filling (0.2 kg per serving)
- Cooking Oil (0.03 litre per serving)

Filled and folded in-house, fried to order in small batches.
