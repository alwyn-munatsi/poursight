# Rice & Gravy

**Category:** Sadza & Sides  
**Price:** $3.50

Steamed rice with a side of gravy - a lighter alternative to sadza.

## Ingredients
- Rice (0.2 kg per serving)
- Gravy Powder (0.03 kg per serving)

## Preparation

Rice is steamed in batches through service; gravy is made fresh from pan drippings.
