# T-Bone Steak

**Category:** Grill  
**Price:** $9.50

A thick-cut T-bone of Zimbabwean farm beef, grilled over open coals to order.

## Ingredients
- Beef Steak Cuts (0.35 kg per serving)
- Cooking Oil (0.02 litre per serving)

## Preparation

Grilled rare, medium, or well done on request, rested briefly, then plated with a side of your choice.
