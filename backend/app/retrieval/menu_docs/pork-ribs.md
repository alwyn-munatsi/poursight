# Pork Ribs

**Category:** Grill  
**Price:** $8.50

A full rack of pork ribs, slow-basted in a sticky peri-peri glaze and finished on the grill.

## Ingredients
- Pork Ribs (raw) (0.45 kg per serving)
- Peri-Peri Sauce (0.03 litre per serving)

## Preparation

Slow-cooked then finished over open coals with a final glaze of peri-peri sauce.
