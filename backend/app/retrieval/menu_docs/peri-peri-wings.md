# Peri-Peri Wings

**Category:** Starter  
**Price:** $4.50

Chicken wings tossed in peri-peri sauce and grilled — a popular starter to share.

## Ingredients
- Chicken Quarters (0.3 kg per serving)
- Peri-Peri Sauce (0.04 litre per serving)

## Preparation

Grilled first, then tossed in sauce just before serving so they stay crisp.
