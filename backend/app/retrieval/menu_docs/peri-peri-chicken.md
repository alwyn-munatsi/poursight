# Peri-Peri Chicken

**Category:** Grill  
**Price:** $7.00

Chicken basted repeatedly in housemade peri-peri sauce while grilling — spicier than the Grilled Chicken Quarter.

## Ingredients
- Chicken Quarters (0.4 kg per serving)
- Peri-Peri Sauce (0.05 litre per serving)

## Preparation

Basted three times during grilling for a sticky, spicy finish. Ask for mild if the standard sauce is too hot.
