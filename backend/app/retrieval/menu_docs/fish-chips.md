# Fish & Chips

**Category:** Grill  
**Price:** $7.50

Imported hake fillet, battered and fried, served with a portion of chips. The fish is imported frozen since Bindura is landlocked, which is why it carries the thinnest margin on the menu.

## Ingredients
- Fish Fillet (imported) (0.3 kg per serving)
- Potatoes (0.3 kg per serving)
- Cooking Oil (0.04 litre per serving)

## Preparation

Battered fresh to order and deep fried, served with hand-cut chips and a lemon wedge.
