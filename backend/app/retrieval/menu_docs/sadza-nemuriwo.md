# Sadza neMuriwo

**Category:** Sadza & Sides  
**Price:** $4.00

Sadza served with sauteed covo/rape (a leafy green also called muriwo) - a lighter vegetarian option.

## Ingredients
- Maize Meal (0.25 kg per serving)
- Covo/Rape (Muriwo) (0.2 kg per serving)
- Cooking Oil (0.02 litre per serving)

## Preparation

The greens are sauteed with onion and a little cooking oil, served alongside freshly stirred sadza.
